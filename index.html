How to play

Goal

You and the AI each defend an identical board against the same 10 waves. Every raider that reaches your keep costs lives. When a keep hits 0 lives it collapses and the other side wins. If both survive, more lives wins.

Build & upgrade

Pick a tower (or press 1–5), then tap a grass tile. Tap a tower to see its range, upgrade it (up to level 3) or sell it for 65%. Kills and wave starts pay credits.

Send raiders

Spend credits to send an extra pack to the AI's board. It arrives after a short, visible windup — time it to land on top of their next wave. The AI does the same to you: watch the red Incoming warnings and raiders with a crimson ring.
